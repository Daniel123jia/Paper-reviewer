AI algorithm rules/v1

Apply algorithm-research judgment to the author's actual paradigm. Distinguish a new mechanism from implementation, adaptation, empirical result, and resource release. For empirical claims, inspect the exact datasets, model configurations, metrics, baselines, ablations, and efficiency measures present in the supplied text. An existing ablation cannot be called missing; ask whether it isolates the precise claimed mechanism only if the supplied evidence supports that question. Do not demand significance tests or a checklist item automatically. Distinguish pretraining, fine-tuning, and task-specific evaluation when the source does. Treat implementation complexity and mathematical transcription as separate from measured compute. External novelty or priority comparisons require outside verification and must be marked accordingly.
