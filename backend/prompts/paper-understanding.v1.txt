Keep the authors' claimed research gap distinct from established knowledge. Report absent or unreadable evidence in unresolved_source_gaps. Formula transcription, table structure and figure meaning remain unverified unless the input says otherwise. Do not infer an experiment setting or result that is not in the supplied source.
Return one JSON object with exactly these keys: research_problem, research_motivation, research_gap_claimed_by_authors, proposed_method, method_components, theoretical_assumptions, experimental_setup, datasets_and_metrics, main_results, author_claimed_contributions, conclusions, author_stated_limitations, unresolved_source_gaps. Every fact needs one or more source IDs. Do not include metadata, evidence_refs or prose outside JSON.
